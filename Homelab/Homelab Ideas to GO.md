### Gotta Explore
Homepage / Heimdall / Homarr
Portainer

Dokploy - open-source alt to netlify, vercel, can install on machine to deploy apps like in vercel

Vaultwarden: A lightweight, resource-friendly, open-source implementation of the Bitwarden server API (Bitwarden is a popular password manager). Hosting this gives you a premium password manager for free, with your data under your control. You can still use the official Bitwarden apps and extensions[](https://dbtechreviews.com/2025/08/27/5-self-hosted-apps-i-use-every-day-in-my-homelab/).

Jellyfin / Plex / Emby: The crown jewels of many homelabs. These media servers let you organize all your movies, TV shows, and music into a personal Netflix-like streaming service[](https://therobbiedavis.com/my-top-10-favorite-self-hosted-apps/#/portal/#/portal/signin). Jellyfin is 100% free and open-source, while Plex is more polished but has moved toward a cloud-centric model[](https://www.xda-developers.com/these-self-hosted-apps-cater-to-my-data-hoarding-needs/)[](https://www.howtogeek.com/open-source-app-replaced-subscriptions/).

Overseerr / Requestrr: Media request management apps. They let family or friends request movies or shows, which you can then approve, making your *arr stack (like Sonarr and Radarr) fully automated[](https://therobbiedavis.com/my-top-10-favorite-self-hosted-apps/#/portal/#/portal/signin).

Romm: A self-hosted game manager for retro game ROMs. It provides metadata and artwork to turn a folder of ROMs into an elegant digital library for your classic games[](https://therobbiedavis.com/my-top-10-favorite-self-hosted-apps/#/portal/#/portal/signin).

Immich: An open-source, feature-rich, and very polished Google Photos alternative with a great UI, AI search capabilities, and automatic mobile backups[](https://www.xda-developers.com/these-self-hosted-apps-cater-to-my-data-hoarding-needs/)[](https://tech.yahoo.com/apps/articles/stop-paying-subscriptions-self-host-123014277.html).

Nextcloud / OpenCloud: An all-in-one productivity suite and file sync tool. It can replace Dropbox, Google Drive, and even Google Docs with its built-in office suite[](https://github.com/MrMikeAde/Self-Hosted-Alternatives)[](https://tech.yahoo.com/apps/articles/stop-paying-subscriptions-self-host-123014277.html).

Paperless-ngx: The go-to document management system. Throw all your receipts, bills, and letters in, and it uses OCR to make everything searchable, taggable, and organized[](https://app.daily.dev/posts/10-quality-of-life-services-i-self-host-on-my-home-lab-v4hijzusz).

Authentik / Authelia: Add a security layer to your self-hosted apps with Single Sign-On (SSO) and Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). Authentik is a modern identity provider, while Authelia is a more lightweight, straightforward alternative[](https://therobbiedavis.com/my-top-10-favorite-self-hosted-apps/#/portal/#/portal/signin).

Home Assistant: The de facto standard for open-source home automation. It can tie together thousands of different devices from a single, local interface without relying on the cloud[](https://app.daily.dev/posts/10-quality-of-life-services-i-self-host-on-my-home-lab-v4hijzusz)[](https://github.com/akmalovaa/homelab-iac/blob/main/README.md).

Ollama + Open WebUI: A powerful combo to run large language models (like Llama, Mistral) completely locally on your own hardware, giving you a private ChatGPT-like experience[](https://github.com/akmalovaa/homelab-iac/blob/main/README.md).

Gluetun: A lightweight VPN client for Docker containers[](https://therobbiedavis.com/my-top-10-favorite-self-hosted-apps/#/portal/#/portal/signin). It's the clean way to route specific apps (like torrent clients) through a VPN without setting up a full VM. (More and more popular for privacy-focused setups)

Pangolin / SWAG: Newer players in the reverse proxy space. Pangolin offers streamlined VPS tunneling, while SWAG bundles an Nginx proxy with built-in SSL and fail2ban[](https://selfh.st/post/2025-favorite-new-apps-so-far/?ref=dailydev)[](https://therobbiedavis.com/my-top-10-favorite-self-hosted-apps/#/portal/#/portal/signin).

Colanode: An open-source alternative to Notion, but with real-time chat built-in, aiming to be an all-in-one tool for documentation and team communication[](https://selfh.st/post/2025-favorite-new-apps-so-far/?ref=dailydev).

[Changedetection.io](https://changedetection.io/): A tool that monitors any webpage and sends you a notification the moment it changes, useful for tracking product restocks or news updates[](https://github.com/upioneer/homelab).

Tinyauth: An ultra-simple authentication middleware for those who want basic protection without the complexity of Authentik[](https://selfh.st/post/2025-favorite-new-apps-so-far/?ref=dailydev).

LoggiFly: A lightweight service that monitors your logs and generates notifications based on specific patterns, a great, simpler alternative to heavy monitoring tools[](https://selfh.st/post/2025-favorite-new-apps-so-far/?ref=dailydev).
