## Purpose of the Video

Set clear expectations for what the videos are and how to learn from them, before getting to the main content.

---

## Phase 1: Story and Philosophy (the current playlist)

- The playlist tells the **story of backend engineering**: its philosophies, the big questions, the inner workings, and the collaborations between different components and machines.
- The narration is meant to help you:
    - See the **big picture of a production-grade backend**.
    - Appreciate the concepts that are **abstracted away** by the languages, runtimes, frameworks and libraries used every day.
- This is the **first step towards being a backend engineer with language-agnostic skills** — skills that go beyond frameworks or any particular library used day to day.
- Once the foundations are clear, you start to see the **common patterns behind every backend application** and understand the philosophies and how the dots connect.

---

## Phase 2: Implementation (next playlist)

- Once the foundations are clear, that is the best time to talk about implementations: how all the principles come together to create a complete backend application, and how they look and work in actual code.
- This is where a **language and ecosystem must be chosen**.
- Plan: release **two versions** — one in **Node.js** and one in **Golang**, since those are the two languages the speaker has firsthand experience in and works with every day.
- In this phase (another playlist), each principle is picked and deep-dived in that language and its surrounding ecosystem.
- Most videos of the Phase 1 playlist will have an **associated implementation-specific video** in the next playlist.
- **Example:** one principle covers databases, drivers, migrations and all the concepts surrounding databases that backend engineers deal with day to day. The Node.js and Go playlists will implement that, deep-diving into, say, **Postgres with the JavaScript driver `postgres.js`** or the **Go driver `pgx`**, covering every concept around it.

---

## Phase 3: Production-Level Projects

- This is where **everything comes together**: all concepts, language-specific deep dives and philosophies.
- Build **production-grade projects end to end**, with all industry standards and best practices.
- Quite a few projects will be built, which you can follow along with if you want.

### Outcome

If you take the journey, internalize everything and follow along with all the projects, you should be able to:

- Safely call yourself a **backend engineer**.
- Go out and **build real systems**:
    - Systems that **scale** — starting from zero users and scaling to a million users.
    - Systems that people can **maintain over a long period of time**.
