## 📌 Standard Internship Cycles Overview

- **Summer Internships (May – July/August):** 2–3 months duration. Primary hiring happens **6–9 months in advance**.
    
- **Off-Campus & Winter/6-Month Internships (Jan – June):** Hiring usually happens between **October and January**.

In India, tech companies generally hire Software Engineer (SDE) interns along **two primary tracks**:

1. **Summer Internship (2 Months, May–July):** The most common cycle. Recruiting happens **8 to 10 months in advance** (August to November of the previous year).
    
2. **6-Month / Semester Internship (Jan–June or Jul–Dec):** Popular among final-year students (7th/8th semester). Hiring typically happens **2 to 4 months prior** (August–October for Jan start, or March–May for Jul start).
    

## 🗓️ India Hiring Calendar by Company Tier

### 1. Big Tech & Product Giants (US Multinationals)

_Companies: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Salesforce, Adobe, Atlassian_

- **When Hiring Happens:** **August to October**
    
- **Target Cohort:** 3rd-year B.Tech / Dual Degree students (for 2-month summer internships) & 4th-year students (for 6-month winter internships).
    
- **Process:** On-campus visits at top tier-1/tier-2 colleges usually occur between **August and September**. Off-campus portal applications and open hiring challenges (e.g., _Google Kickstart/Girl Hackathon_, _Microsoft Engage/Foundations_) typically roll out around **July to September**.
    

### 2. Global Investment Banks & Financial Firms

_Companies: Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase (JPMC), Morgan Stanley, Barclays, BNY Mellon, Deutsche Bank, Standard Chartered, American Express, Mastercard_

- **When Hiring Happens:** **July to November**
    
- **Target Cohort:** 3rd-year students for Summer Internships.
    
- **Process:**
    
    - **Goldman Sachs:** Conducts its famous _Engineering Campus Hiring Program_ (off-campus & on-campus) around **July to August**.
        
    - **JPMC:** Runs its _Code for Good_ hackathon around **May to July** as a direct pipeline for SDE internships.
        
    - **Others:** Start campus hiring in **August–October**, with off-campus drives spilling into **December**.
        

### 3. High-Growth Product Companies & Unicorns

_Companies: Flipkart, Meesho, Zepto, InMobi, Glance, ThoughtSpot, Sprinklr, UnifyApps, Bidgely_

- **When Hiring Happens:** **September to January**
    
- **Process:**
    
    - **Flipkart:** Hires via its flagship hackathon, _Flipkart GRiD_, which opens registrations in **July–August**. On-campus visits run from **September to November**.
        
    - **Zepto, Meesho, Sprinklr:** Heavily hire for 6-month SDE internships. They run campus/off-campus hiring drives in **October to December** for January starts, and **March to May** for July starts.
        

### 4. Enterprise Services & Energy

_Companies: Schlumberger (SLB), Kenvue, Tata AIG, Expedia_

- **When Hiring Happens:** **August to December**
    
- **Process:** Primarily visit campus placement drives in **August–November**. Off-campus roles are posted on their career portals on a rolling basis whenever business needs arise.
